Established in 1978, Groza Construction has been involved in some of the most technical craftsmanship on the Monterey Peninsula. Our reputation has grown from the evidence that our people – from the Project Manager to the Apprentice Carpenter – share the desire and passion to build exceptionally. Maintaining the highest level of integrity in all our business transactions has enabled us to build relationships of trust and cooperation among suppliers, subcontractors, architects, interior designers, engineers, and most importantly, our clients.

Our reputation for excellent collaboration has resulted in projects all over the Central Coast including Big Sur, Carmel, Carmel Valley, Pebble Beach, Monterra, Tehama, the Santa Lucia Preserve, Monterey, Pacific Grove, and Santa Cruz.

The core employees of Groza Construction are specialists. Project Managers are proficient in communication, budgeting, and analysis, replete with field experience to bolster their contributions. Foremen are master carpenters with a creative eye that provides insight and tolerates no inefficiency. This leadership results in efficient projects built to the highest standard.

We look forward to collaborating with you on your next design endeavor.

Green building, and a duty to the coast

Here at Groza Construction, we understand the importance of progress and technology in the building industry. We have completed homes that comply with the guidelines and standards of both the USGBC and the Build It Green programs, including the first LEED Platinum residence in Monterey County, and have developed the necessary administrative tools to navigate green projects efficiently in the future. As the demand for certified green homes is growing, the ability to work as a team is invaluable. We pride ourselves on assembling outstanding design-build teams with the abilities to accommodate any style or programmatic requirements.

Our in-house engineers manage our storm water program, which prevents site sediment from damaging sensitive waterways, including the Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuary. Most of our team grew up here, and we have a vested interest in keeping the peninsula safe, clean, and healthy.
