Community Spotlight: Friendship Bench

July 15, 2016

The Groza team loves to support local projects. Eva West, Class president of Tularcitos Elementary, spearheaded this symbolic build to promote bullying awareness and friendship around the school. Groza Construction and Commercial Woodworking supported this project because representative of how we like to operate, we value positive relationships and the community.

Great job Eva and we hope you continue to be a leader in our community. Visit our blog and see what other fun activities Groza Construction is working on.
