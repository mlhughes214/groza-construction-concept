Butterfly House

Type
Residential
Location
Santa Lucia Preserve, near Carmel
Architect
Feldman Architecture
Builder
Groza Construction
  • Three pavilions, each topped by an expressive V-shaped butterfly roof, on a 20,000-acre private preserve.
  • Monterey AIA Chapter Citation Award (January 2014).
  • On the cover of Dwell (April 2014).
  • 2015 American Architecture Award, Chicago Athenaeum Museum (October 2015).
  • Groza has now constructed four houses for Feldman Architecture within the Santa Lucia Preserve (January 2014).
