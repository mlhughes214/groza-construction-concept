Built with Feldman Architecture

“Groza has now constructed four houses for Feldman Architecture within the Santa Lucia Preserve.” Three of those homes, as covered by the press.

Butterfly House

Santa Lucia Preserve, near Carmel

  • Three pavilions, each topped by a V-shaped butterfly roof, on a 20,000-acre private preserve.
  • Wins the Monterey AIA Chapter Citation Award (January 2014).
  • On the cover of Dwell (April 2014).
  • Wins a 2015 American Architecture Award (Chicago Athenaeum Museum).
  • Featured in ArchDaily, Dezeen, Uncrate, Architectural Digest Latino and more. See the full list.

Caterpillar House

A bluff in the Santa Lucia Preserve, Carmel

  • The first LEED Platinum custom home on California’s Central Coast (Build with Rise, September 2018).
  • A low, broad roof sweeps over a simple S-shaped form, blending the house into the landscape (Architecture, December 2012).
  • Builder’s Choice and Ecohome Grand Award coverage (2011).

Ranch OH

A weekend retreat in the Santa Lucia Preserve

  • Designed around mature valley oaks, shaped like a horseshoe, with a traditional ranch-house feel and modern influences.
  • Featured in Dwell (July 2016), Elle Decor Spain (December 2018) and One Kind Design (November 2018).
